Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-20

Skinface AI (com.skinface.ai) is a mobile app that analyzes facial and skin metrics using your device camera.

What we collect

  • Camera frames during a live scan (processed on device for landmarks and skin metrics).
  • When signed in: scan scores, analysis, recommendations, and profile (email, age range, gender preference).
  • Optional scan images stored with your account for up to 24 hours for dashboard/share, then deleted.
  • Subscription status via Apple/Google and RevenueCat.

Retention

Scores and insights are kept while your account exists so you can track progress.

Scan photos / biometric images are deleted within 24 hours. After deletion, history still shows numbers with a placeholder image.

AI processing

Sanitized numeric metrics (not raw photos for routine generation) may be sent to our HTTPS backend for one-time inference (Google Gemini). We do not sell your data.

Your rights

Use Delete Account in the app to remove your data and auth account, or email support@skinface.ai.
